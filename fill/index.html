Your business's own memory.

Verrandi gives your business its own memory: everything from your email, files and records in one place you own, which you can search, understand and ask anything, without changing how anyone works.

  • It reads; it changes nothing. Your mail, files and records stay where they are, in your accounts, unchanged. Nobody else has to use it.
  • Same keys as today. Everyone finds, understands and asks about only what they can already open in your email, drives and systems.
  • Sensitive things stay with the right people. Anything not yet checked for privacy goes only to the owner and to whoever owns where it came from.
  • Made from locked stays locked. When it keeps something new, like a saved chat, it remembers what it was made from; someone else sees it only if they can open all of that. Sharing beyond that is your choice, after it shows you what is private in it.
  • Every fact traceable. What it tells you points back to the source in your own systems, and it says plainly what it did not read.
  • It is yours. It lives in your own database and stays yours, even if you stop using Verrandi.

What happens next

You connect what your business runs on, one source at a time. A few minutes each. You can stop after any one of them, and what is connected already works.

Before you connect anything

There are two kinds of connection, and they behave differently.

Read and remembered

Mail, files, calendar and contacts, and the records of systems the Tool keeps a copy of, like your books, CRM or applicant tracker. Tool reads these and keeps them current, so what is in them becomes part of the memory: what happened, when, who was involved, and how one thing relates to another. New mail is read from the day you connect it; older mail waits until you choose how much of it to read.

Why it matters: this is the kind it can reason over in depth, and connect across. Depth comes from here.

Reached and used

Bank accounts, and any system the Tool does not keep a copy of. Tool reaches these at the moment you ask, so the figure is whatever the system says right then. Reports from your books are asked for at that moment too. Nothing is ever written back.

Why it matters: current, but shallow — it knows only what it went and asked for. The number comes from here.

Most real questions need both: the remembered side for what happened and why, the reached side for what the figure is today.

    4 · The AI labels; a fixed rule decides

    A fixed rule decides who sees each item.

    • The AI labels items as it reads them, for example “salary”, “employment contract” or “sick leave”.
    • Every new item is checked for sensitive topics, like pay, health and personal records, even in a shared mailbox, and kept with the people who should see them.
    • A simple fixed rule then decides who sees it, the same way every time.
    • When in doubt, the item stays with fewer people. An item not yet checked stays with the Owner seat and the person it came from.

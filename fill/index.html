Toolhirekraft

A working memory of your business.

Tool reads what your business already runs on — your mail, your files, your records — and builds one current understanding of it. You can ask it questions and act on what it tells you.

  • It reads. It does not move anything. Your mail, files and records stay where they are, in your accounts, unchanged.
  • Everything it tells you traces back to the source it came from, in your own systems, so you can check it yourself.
  • It lives in your own database and answers to your own logins. Each person reaches only what they already reach at work.

Audit us with infrastructure we don't own. Every claim points back into your accounts, not ours.

What happens next

You connect what your business runs on, one source at a time. A few minutes each. You can stop after any one of them, and what is connected already works.

Before you connect anything

There are two kinds of connection, and they behave differently.

Read and remembered

Mail, files, calendar. Tool reads these and keeps reading them, so what is in them becomes part of the memory: what happened, when, who was involved, and how one thing relates to another.

Why it matters: this is the kind it can reason over in depth, and connect across. Depth comes from here.

Reached and used

QuickBooks, and tools like it. Tool does not copy these. It reaches them at the moment you ask, so the figure is whatever the system says right then.

Why it matters: current, but shallow — it knows only what it went and asked for. The number comes from here.

Most real questions need both: the remembered side for what happened and why, the reached side for what the figure is today.

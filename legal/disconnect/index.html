Disconnecting a system

Applies to Tool. Effective 1 September 2026.

You can disconnect at any time, from either end, without asking us. Disconnecting takes effect immediately.

From inside Tool

Open your sources, find the system, and choose to disconnect it. Tool discards the stored authorization for that system and stops reading it.

From QuickBooks Online

You can also revoke access from Intuit's side, which works even if you cannot reach Tool. In QuickBooks Online, open the settings menu, go to Apps, find this app in your connected apps, and disconnect it. Intuit revokes the authorization immediately and no further reading is possible.

What happens to your data

QuickBooks accounting records are read live and are not copied into our storage, so once the connection ends there is nothing further to remove.

For systems whose content is stored — such as email and documents — disconnecting stops further reading but does not by itself delete what was already stored. To have that deleted, write to privacy@hirekraft.ai and we will remove it. Because the database belongs to you, you may also delete it directly.

Reconnecting

Reconnecting is the same route as connecting: open your sources and choose the system. You will be asked to authorize again, and a new authorization replaces the old one.

If something does not work

Write to support@hirekraft.ai. If you cannot reach us and want access stopped immediately, revoke it from the source system directly using the steps above — that does not depend on us.

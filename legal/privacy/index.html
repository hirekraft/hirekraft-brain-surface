Privacy Policy

Applies to Tool. Effective 1 September 2026.

The short version: your data stays in a database you own. We do not copy it out, we do not sell it, we do not train on it, and we do not hold a key that reaches it while you are logged out.

Who we are

Tool is built and operated by AN Software LLC ("we", "us"). Contact us at privacy@hirekraft.ai.

This policy covers Tool, including its connection to QuickBooks Online and to any other system a customer chooses to connect.

Where your data lives

Each customer's data is held in a database instance dedicated to that customer. Content from connected systems — email, documents, calendar entries, accounting records — is stored there and nowhere else. We do not maintain a combined store of customer content across customers.

Some connected systems are read live rather than copied. QuickBooks Online is one of these: accounting records are read from Intuit at the moment a question is asked and are not written into our storage.

What we access, and when

Tool acts on behalf of a signed-in person, using that person's own authorization to each connected system. A person reaches through Tool exactly what they could reach directly in the source system, and no more. When nobody is signed in, Tool is not reading anything.

Access credentials — including OAuth tokens for QuickBooks Online — are held encrypted in the customer's own database. Token renewal passes through a service we operate; the business records themselves do not.

Intuit and QuickBooks Online data

Specific to the QuickBooks Online connection:

  • We request only the accounting scope, and only for companies a customer explicitly connects.
  • Accounting data is read on demand to answer a question or prepare a proposed change. It is not copied into our storage, not indexed, and not retained after the request completes.
  • We do not write to a customer's books without that customer approving the specific change first.
  • We do not disclose QuickBooks data to any third party, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.
  • Disconnecting removes our authorization. See how to disconnect.

What we never do

  • We do not sell customer data, and we do not share it with data brokers or advertisers.
  • We do not use customer content to train machine-learning models.
  • We do not read customer content for our own purposes.

Service providers

Tool runs on infrastructure operated by third parties, including database and cloud hosting providers and the provider of the language model that performs reasoning. Content is sent to the reasoning provider only as needed to answer a request, under terms that prohibit retention and training on that content. These providers act on our instructions and may not use customer data for their own purposes.

Retention and deletion

Content stays for as long as the customer keeps the source connected. Disconnecting a source stops further reading; a customer may ask us to delete what was previously stored, and we will do so. Because the database belongs to the customer, a customer may also delete it directly. Operational logs that record that a request occurred, without its content, are kept for a limited period for security and reliability.

Security

Credentials are stored encrypted. Access is denied by default and granted only through checks that resolve the signed-in person's identity from their own session. We monitor continuously for misconfiguration that could widen access and treat any such finding as an incident.

Your rights

Customers may ask what is held about them, ask for a copy, ask for correction, or ask for deletion, by writing to privacy@hirekraft.ai. We respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live you may also have the right to complain to a data protection authority.

Children

Tool is a business product and is not directed to anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the effective date above and notify connected customers before the change takes effect.
